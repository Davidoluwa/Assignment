Parallax Capture

Record one short clip of your head turning. The page pulls still frames out of it and sends them to the Parallax pipeline, which fuses them into a single 3D shape of your face.

01 How to move

  • 8 to 10 seconds. Start straight, turn slowly left, slowly right, back to centre, then nod up and down once.
  • Phone stays still. Prop it up or hold it steady at eye level, about an arm's length away. Only your head moves.
  • Even light on your face. No window behind you. Glasses off if you can; keep a neutral expression.
  • Both turns matter. Left and right give depth; the nod is what lets the system measure real scale.
